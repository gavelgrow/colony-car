What does it cost to hire Colony?

Nothing up front. We work on a contingency fee: no attorney fee unless we win.* You may still be responsible for case costs, as set out in a written fee agreement. The free review does not create an attorney-client relationship.

How long do I have to make a claim in California?

Many injury lawsuits must be filed within two years of the crash. Claims against a city, county, or other public entity can have a deadline as short as six months. Call before you assume you still have time.

I was a passenger. Do I still have a claim?

Often yes. Passengers are rarely the at-fault driver. Coverage can sit on more than one policy. We review that on the first call — still no attorney fee unless we win.*

What if the other driver has no insurance, or fled?

You may still have a path through your own uninsured / underinsured motorist coverage, and through any other policy that applies. Hit-and-run and UM claims are slower. They are not automatically hopeless.
