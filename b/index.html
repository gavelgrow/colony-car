Do I pay anything to start my case?

No. We work on a contingency fee, which means there is no attorney fee unless we win your case. Our fee is a percentage of your settlement or verdict. You are responsible for case costs we advance, such as medical record fees, even if there is no recovery. The fee agreement explains this before you sign.

Does sharing the blame end my claim?

No. In California, a driver who was partly at fault can still recover under pure comparative negligence. The amount is reduced by that driver’s percentage of fault.

What is the time limit after a car accident in Los Angeles?

For most car accident injury claims, California sets a two-year limit for filing a lawsuit, counted from the crash. There are exceptions. If a city, county or state agency was at fault, a written government claim has to be filed much sooner, within months. Ask about deadlines promptly.

What if I was in an Uber or Lyft crash?

Rideshare crashes can involve the driver’s personal policy and the coverage Uber or Lyft carries. Which one applies usually depends on whether the app was on and whether a ride was underway.

Can we do this in Spanish?

Yes. Our intake staff speak Spanish; they are not attorneys. Ask for Spanish when you call, or tell us when we contact you. Nuestro personal de admisión habla español.

What we left out on purpose

The giant dollar figure, the verdict cards with client stories, the press logos, “largest firm” language and a founder video. Those are Morgan & Morgan’s proof, and they’re either claims Colony can’t make or things Colony doesn’t have. Case results and success percentages are the riskiest claims under California advertising rules, so we didn’t substitute anything like them. A short video from Kevin would be the closest honest answer to their video, and it’s on the list below.
